# Round robin CPU matchup results

On 26 November 2026, the browser combat engine completed 8,200 CPU-versus-CPU matches between the 41 fighters. **ii!** led with 328 wins in 400 matches (82%) and won 38 of 40 series. **Olivar** and **Kiln** finished second and third. **Kirbi** and **Zunchy** had the fewest wins.

## Schedule and scoring

All 820 distinct pairs played ten matches. Each pair played twice in each of the five standard arenas with starting sides reversed. Every fighter played 400 matches, with 80 per arena and 200 per starting side. Both slots used the built-in CPU. Standard rules supplied 3 stocks, a 3-minute limit, a 120 Hz simulation step, and the normal arena and Superstar mechanics.

Rank uses wins plus half a point per draw. Cumulative stock differential breaks a tie in score. Stock differential is the sum of a fighter's remaining stocks minus the opponent's across all matches. Series records count the number of opponents each fighter beat, lost to, or tied over ten matches. The tournament had 62 tied series, 146 10–0 series, and zero drawn matches. Of the 42 time-limit decisions, 28 were settled by stocks and 14 by damage.
