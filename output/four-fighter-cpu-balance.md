# CPU matchup ranking against Rivet Bramble Vesper and Kiln

On 26 November 2026, the browser combat engine completed 1,540 CPU-versus-CPU matches across 154 distinct fighter pairings, plus 40 mirror matches. SuperLibroGirl, Cerbero, and Olivar won their series against all four reference fighters. Kiln was the strongest reference fighter, winning 285 of 370 matches against the 37 common opponents and 29 of 30 against the other three references.

## Match rules and schedule

Each pair played twice on each standard arena and started once on each side. Matches used the browser game's 3 stocks, 3-minute limit, 120 Hz simulation step, and standard arena and Superstar rules. Both slots used the built-in CPU with an empty controller input map. The 6 pairings among the reference fighters each contributed one 10-match series.

The 37 challengers are ranked by wins across their 40 matches, with cumulative stock differential as the tiebreaker. Stock differential is the sum of the player's remaining stocks minus the opponent's. 36 matches reached the time limit, including 35 between distinct fighters. All matches produced a winner.
