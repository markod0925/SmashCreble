# Rift Trail Campaign Structure

Isole alla deriva is a five-act Smash Creble campaign for ages 6–9. Each act has three levels. The first introduces an action safely, the second uses it alongside enemies, and the third uses it to restore a connection on the world map. The acts follow a fixed order. Optional routes and rescues give players room to explore within that order.

## Act I — Cloudbreak Foundry

| Level | Goal | Local co-op |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. The Lights of Cloudbreak | Step on a floor switch to power the foundry, hit the yellow door switch, and walk through the green door. | Any player can activate either switch. The whole party can stay on the continuous floor. |
| 2. The Jammed Gate | Strike a crate to slide it into a stop. A sentry leaves an opening after its charge. | One player can draw the sentry away while another moves the crate. |
| 3. The Foundry Bridge | Reach the bridge switch while the keeper recovers from a clearly telegraphed slam. Cross the lowered bridge. | One player can engage the keeper while another takes the walkway. Clearing the level restores Cloudbreak as the campaign base. |

## Act II — Stormcoil Array

| Level | Goal | Local co-op |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4. Follow the Windsocks | Use windsocks to read the gusts and take the sheltered platforms. A lower walkway catches players pushed off course. | A player on the lower route can call a lift for the group. |
| 5. The Side Passage | Ride the lifts to the exit. An optional alcove contains a fighter rescue and a wind stabilizer. | The team can split briefly to reach the rescue. The routes meet before the exit. |
| 6. The Winch | Use a lift to reach the winch, then strike while it leans out to repair a stop. | A player can hold the lift in place while a teammate climbs. The lift becomes a shortcut on the map. |

## Act III — Glass Gardens

| Level | Goal | Local co-op |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7. The Cracked Floor | Break a visibly cracked panel. A safe garden bed catches the first drop. | A side ramp and the panel both lead to the lower path. |
| 8. The Drone Greenhouse | Change height when a drone marks a lane. Breaking a panel reveals a sheltered route. | Players can distract the drone and open the route in parallel. |
| 9. The Sluice Keeper | Break a panel when the keeper lands on it after a charge. Open the sluice to restore water to the gardens. | One player can draw the keeper onto the panel while another strikes it. Solo players can wait for the end of its charge. |

## Act IV — GDMR Bridge

| Level | Goal | Local co-op |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10. The Giant Balance | Shift weight on a seesaw platform to reach an exit. Low barriers make the first practice area safe. | Players can shift weight together. A movable weight gives solo players the same option. |
| 11. The Sliding Load | Slide a counterweight into a rail while a sentry patrols the approach. A catch keeps the weight in place. | One player can keep the ramp clear while another moves the load. |
| 12. The Bridge Keeper | Tilt the platform during the keeper's recovery to reach and release the bridge lock. | Players can act from the platform or strike the counterweight from the walkway. Lowering the bridge opens the core. |

## Act V — The Regulator Core

| Level | Goal | Local co-op |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 13. The Relay Room | Restore relays in short rooms that revisit wind, glass, and counterweights. Each restored relay records a checkpoint. | Players can alternate between reaching the highlighted relay and engaging enemies. |
| 14. The Final Climb | Use lifts and sheltered alcoves to climb through the core. | A player who reaches the next alcove can open a walkway for teammates. |
| 15. The Regulator | Restore the arena controls during recovery windows, then hit the exposed core switch to reconnect the islands. | Anyone can activate a control. A team wipe restarts the current phase with earlier phases cleared. |

## Difficulty and progression

- Easy is the default. Show one short objective and mark its target in the arena. Introduce hazards through visible motion and shape, with audio as a second cue.
- Give players unlimited recoveries from safe checkpoints. Preserve solved objectives when someone falls. Bring players who leave the cooperative camera back to a safe point near the group.
- Let any teammate advance a shared objective. Design each required action so one player can perform it alone. Allow players to join or leave between levels.
- Keep the main route open to every fighter. Optional rescues add campaign choices. A team may equip one situational module when the module system arrives.
- A later **Hard** option should play like standard difficulty. Stronger hazards and shorter openings increase the challenge while checkpoint rules stay consistent. The option belongs on the map between levels and uses the same campaign progress.
